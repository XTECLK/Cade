# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome people of every background and experience, give and take feedback gracefully, and
keep to the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and other unwelcome behaviour aren't accepted in issues, pull requests,
reviews or any other space for this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this, and may ban people who do. To report a problem, use
[GitHub's report tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or
contact a maintainer privately; reports are kept confidential.
