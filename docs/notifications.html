Set up
Notifications

Who hears what.

When someone changes a task's people, pull requests or status from the card, a suggestion or the standup, Cade posts one message in the task's thread: what changed. What it means for the people it names, such as “@rex, could you pick this up?”, isn't posted for you: only you see it, as a draft to Send, Edit first or leave with Not now, since you may know it isn't needed, or want to say it your own way. A typed command, such as @Cade assign or @Cade pr add, gets a ✅ on your message instead of the change log, and the same draft.

First, the change log

A line per change, who made it and what it was: “@ann assigned @rex, @bob as owner.” The thread keeps a log of every change. Someone changing their own part reads as “@rex assigned themselves as owner.”, and isn't asked or told anything, since they made the change. When a change comes from a suggestion, the people its message mentioned were notified by it already: they're named rather than mentioned (“@ann assigned Tom as tester.”), and not asked again.

Then, what it means for them, if you send it

Offered to whoever made the change, privately, in the task's thread. Send posts it there, led by who it's from, “@ann sent:”, as your AI agent's messages are; Edit opens it in a form to change first, mentions and all; Not now, or leaving it, sends nothing. A line each: “@rex, @bob, could you pick this up?” or “@tom, there's no need to test this anymore.” One note for each person, not each change: someone added as owner and reviewer is asked “@rex, could you pick this up and review web/12 …”, and people asked the same share a note.

Offered only when there's something to do now

  • Owners are asked when they're assigned while the task is To do or In progress. Past that, their part is over.
  • Reviewers are asked when the task moves to In review, or when they're assigned while it's In review, or when a PR is linked while it's In review. They're asked about the open PRs, or to review it when there are none. Before In review they wait: the task moving there asks them.
  • Testers are asked when the task moves to In testing, or when they're assigned and it's In testing already.
  • Those removed are told there's no need anymore only if they'd have been asked.
