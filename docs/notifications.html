Set up
Notifications

Who hears what.

When someone changes a task's people, pull requests or status from the card, a suggestion or the standup, Cade posts one message in the task's thread: what changed, then what it means for the people it names. There's nothing to confirm, and one change is one message, so everyone is notified once. A typed command, such as @Cade assign or @Cade pr add, gets a ✅ on your message instead of the change log; only the requests are posted.

First, the change log

A line per change, who made it and what it was: “@ann assigned @rex, @bob as owner.” The thread keeps a log of every change. Someone changing their own part reads as “@rex assigned themselves as owner.”, and isn't asked or told anything, since they made the change. When a change comes from a suggestion, the people its message mentioned were notified by it already: they're named rather than mentioned (“@ann assigned Tom as tester.”), and not asked again.

Then, what it means for them

A line each: “@rex, @bob, could you pick this up when you get a chance? Thanks!” or “@tom, there's no need to test this anymore. Thanks!” One note for each person, not each change: someone added as owner and reviewer is asked “@rex, could you pick this up and review web/12 …”, and people asked the same share a note.

Only when there's something to do now

  • Owners are asked when they're assigned while the task is To do or In progress. Past that, their part is over.
  • Reviewers are asked when the task moves to In review, or when they're assigned while it's In review, or when a PR is linked while it's In review. They're asked about the pending PRs (not yet sent for approval), or to review it when there are none. Before In review they wait: the task moving there asks them.
  • Testers are asked when the task moves to In testing, or when they're assigned and it's In testing already.
  • Those removed are told there's no need anymore only if they'd have been asked.

Changing status

Asking testers

Moving to In testing, however it's done

Thread
@ann set the status to In testing.
@tom, could you give this a test when you get a chance? Thanks!

The same from @Cade status and its picker, the task card's Edit, suggestions and standup. A status typed in the command, like @Cade status testing, gets a ✅ on your message, with only the testers' request posted. The @Cade status picker becomes the change log; when testers are asked, it's replaced by a new reply instead, since Slack doesn't notify anyone about an edited message.

Moving to In review, however it's done

Thread
@ann set the status to In review.
@mia, could you review web/12 when you get a chance? Thanks!

Every reviewer assigned earlier is asked, those just added too, about the PRs pending. With no PRs they're asked to review it, and when every PR has been sent for approval there's nothing left to review, so no one is asked. Owners aren't asked again: it's past their part.
